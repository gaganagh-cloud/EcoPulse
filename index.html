Why EcoPulse Succeeds Without Costly Hardware or ESP32 Devices

1. Citizen Crowdsourcing

Every student, resident, and municipal employee has a GPS-enabled camera phone, creating an instant distributed sensor network.

2. Algorithmic Triaging

Intelligent heuristics compute flow loss, prioritize severe leaks, and bundle proximate reports to avoid duplicates.

3. Rapid Ward Action

Direct dispatch to municipal ward plumbers and campus maintenance, closing tickets in hours instead of weeks.
